Research & Doctoral Work

My research explores spherical $s$-distance sets and the intersection of degree $d$ hypersurfaces with non-degenerate Hermitian varieties in projective spaces[cite: 3].

Doctoral Thesis Summary

Thesis Title: A result of s-distance sets and some results towards Edoukou's conjecture on intersection of Hermitian threefolds[cite: 1]

Part 1 — Spherical $s$-distance sets: We studied the cardinality of spherical $s$-distance sets and derived a new upper bound generalizing Gerzon's bound under the condition that the sum of inner products over distinct pairs is zero, comparing it with previously established bounds[cite: 3].

Part 2 — Edoukou's Conjecture: We investigated Edoukou's conjecture regarding the maximum number of $\mathbb{F}_{q^2}$-rational points in the intersection of a degree $d$ threefold with a non-degenerate Hermitian threefold in $\mathbb{P}^4(\mathbb{F}_{q^2})$[cite: 3]. We verified the conjecture for $d=3$ ($q \ge 7$), gave an independent proof for $d=2$, and generalized this to confirm the Edoukou-Ling-Xing conjecture for cubic hypersurfaces in $\mathbb{P}^n(\mathbb{F}_{q^2})$ ($n \ge 4$)[cite: 3].
