Research Focus & Doctoral Thesis

My research lies in Algebraic Coding Theory, Finite Geometry, Algebraic Geometry, Combinatorics, and Cryptography. In my doctoral work at IIT Hyderabad, I explored spherical $s$-distance sets and the intersection properties of degree $d$ hypersurfaces with non-degenerate Hermitian varieties in projective spaces $\mathbb{P}^n(\mathbb{F}_{q^2})$.

Read Doctoral Thesis Summary

Thesis Title: A result of $s$-distance sets and some results towards Edoukou's conjecture on intersection of Hermitian threefolds


My doctoral work is divided into two main parts:

  1. Spherical $s$-distance sets: We derived a new upper bound on the cardinality of spherical $s$-distance sets by generalizing Gerzon's bound under the condition that the sum of inner products over distinct pairs vanishes, comparing it with classical bounds.
  2. Edoukou's Conjecture & Hermitian Varieties: We investigated Edoukou's 2009 conjecture regarding the maximum number of $\mathbb{F}_{q^2}$-rational points in the intersection of a degree $d$ threefold with a non-degenerate Hermitian threefold in $\mathbb{P}^4(\mathbb{F}_{q^2})$. We verified the conjecture for cubic threefolds ($d=3$) for $q \ge 7$ and provided an independent proof for $d=2$. Furthermore, we generalized this to higher dimensions for $n \ge 4$, confirming the Edoukou-Ling-Xing conjecture for cubic hypersurfaces.
