Research & Doctoral Thesis

My doctoral research investigates spherical $s$-distance sets and the intersection properties of degree $d$ hypersurfaces with non-degenerate Hermitian varieties in projective spaces.

Doctoral Thesis Summary

Title: A result of s-distance sets and some results towards Edoukou's conjecture on intersection of Hermitian threefolds

1. Spherical $s$-distance sets: We derived a new upper bound generalizing Gerzon's bound on the cardinality of spherical $s$-distance sets under the condition that the sum of inner products over distinct pairs vanishes.

2. Edoukou's Conjecture: We investigated Edoukou's conjecture regarding the maximum number of $\mathbb{F}_{q^2}$-rational points in the intersection of a degree $d$ threefold with a non-degenerate Hermitian threefold in $\mathbb{P}^4(\mathbb{F}_{q^2})$. We verified the conjecture for $d=3$ ($q \ge 7$), gave an independent proof for $d=2$, and extended these results to cubic hypersurfaces in higher dimensions ($n \ge 4$).
