About me

I am a postdoctoral fellow at Indian Institute of Technology Bombay (IITB), where I work on research in Code-based Cryptography. My work focuses on Algebraic geometry and its applications to linear error-correcting codes.

My research combines Algebraic geometry over finite fields, Linear codes and Code-based Cryptography. I am particularly interested in the geometric approach of linear codes arising from different kinds of algebraic varieties over finite fields.

In addition to research, I also enjoy exploring nature, watching movies, sharing research tools, and collaborating with researchers across disciplines.
